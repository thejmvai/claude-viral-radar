    The 90% cadence

    Touches counted back from policy expiry
    1. T-60days
      Heads-up and data check.

      Confirm drivers, address and any modifications. Tell them you are shopping their renewal now. Early contact is the biggest single lever on retention.

    2. T-30days
      Quote in hand.

      Send the renewal premium with NCD shown. If the price rose, re-market with a second insurer before they do it themselves.

    3. T-14days
      Close.

      Call, don't text. Handle the price objection, offer instalments, and ask for the go-ahead.

    4. T-7days
      Urgency.

      Remind them road tax renewal needs valid cover. Book a time to settle payment.

    5. T-3days
      Last call before lapse.

      Escalate to the salesperson who sold them the car. A familiar voice converts fence-sitters.

    6. T-0expiry
      Expiry day.

      Final reminder that cover ends at midnight. Anything still open becomes a win-back.

    7. T+30after
      Win-back window.

      Lapsed clients stay in the call queue for 30 days. After that, record them as lost with a reason so you can fix the cause.
